# LVIO_exp

A controlled ablation study of two LiDAR-inertial odometry systems, **SE(3)-LVIO** and
**lightning-lm**, on GrandTour / COMFORT data. Every result is pre-registered, reproducible, and
measured against the run-to-run noise floor.
